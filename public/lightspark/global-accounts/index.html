Agentic accounts

Your customers get a dollar account. Your business gets a revenue engine. AI agents are next. Every company is building them. Almost none can let them touch money safely. Most agent payment products are workflow logic wrapped around an API. Useful, but replaceable. Global Accounts is different: the account itself is the operating environment, so permissions live where the money lives.

What everyone else will shipPermissions at the application layer01
What Lightspark shipsPermissions enforced at the account layer02

The agent gets a pocket. Not the keys. Scoped authority over funding sources, rails, payees, and thresholds, plus a full audit trail and instant revocation. The wallet enforces the policy, not the agent.

Agents operate inside the accountNot calling in from outside through an API. Operating natively inside the wallet.01
Bounded pockets with scoped authorityFunding sources, payees, rails, and limits are all defined per agent.02
Wallet enforces policyAgents can't escalate privileges or exceed their scope03
One control plane for everythingBalances, cards, payouts, and agent permissions use the same infrastructure.04
Full audit trail and instant revocationEvery agent action is traceable. Authority can be revoked in real time.05
